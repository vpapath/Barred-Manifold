# AI Collaboration Disclosure

This research programme was developed with substantial assistance from Claude (Anthropic), an AI assistant.

## What Claude contributed

- Mathematical derivation and formalization, including working through the universe hierarchy
  formalization of the sexuation formulas and the logical time connection
- Agda code development, debugging, and typechecking
- Structural drafting of all three papers
- LaTeX typesetting
- Literature search and synthesis

## What the author contributed

- The originating theoretical framework (thesis: *The Barred Manifold*, Zenodo 2026)
- All interpretive and clinical arguments
- The research direction and editorial decisions at each stage
- The intellectual judgment about what is worth pursuing
- All final decisions about what the papers claim

## Why this disclosure matters

The papers in this repository make formal claims — machine-verified results in Agda. Those results
are correct regardless of how they were produced. The Agda type checker does not care whether
the code was written by a human or assisted by AI; it either typechecks or it does not.

The theoretical claims — that the universe hierarchy formalization satisfies Corfield's RSI
permutation criterion, that logical time and sexual difference are formally unified, that
constitutive directionality is absent from simplicial type theory — are argued in the papers
and are the author's responsibility.

The disclosure is made in the spirit of the author's broader intellectual commitment to honesty
about the conditions of production of this work.

## Note on the open conjecture

The constitutive directed univalence conjecture in Paper 03 was identified through our
collaborative mathematical exploration. It is stated as an open problem precisely because
neither the author nor Claude could prove it. Its resolution requires either the full CTT
metatheory or a new proof assistant — work that goes beyond what this collaboration produced.
